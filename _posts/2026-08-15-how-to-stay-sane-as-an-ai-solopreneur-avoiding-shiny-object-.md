If you’re chasing every new wrapper released on Product Hunt, you aren't building a business; you’re just curating a graveyard of abandoned API keys.

> **⚡ TL;DR**
> * Shiny Object Syndrome is the silent killer of MRR.
> * Pick one core stack and ignore everything else for 30 days.
> * **Skip this if:** You genuinely enjoy wasting money on subscriptions you don't use.

## 🧠 The Reality Check
The biggest myth in the indie dev community is that "AI-native" means you need a new tech stack every Tuesday. You don’t need the latest LLM-integrated CRM to close your first ten customers. Most "AI-powered" tools are just a thin layer over GPT-4 with a bloated UI. If your current workflow is slow, fix the process before you automate it with a $40/month subscription.

## ⚙️ The Solopreneur Playbook
1. Audit your tools and cancel anything you haven't opened in seven days.
2. Define one primary revenue-generating action for your business this month.
3. Only adopt a new AI tool if it directly accelerates that one action.
4. Set a "Hard No" rule: No new subscriptions until the current tool is profitable.
5. Review your stack on the first of every month and purge the dead weight.

## 📉 The Catch
The catch is that this takes immense discipline. It’s boring to stick to a basic text editor or a standard database when everyone else is playing with AI agents that "write code for you." You will feel like you’re falling behind. You aren't. You’re just not distracted.

**The Builders' Math**
*   **Cost:** $30/mo for a "productivity agent" you barely use.
*   **Time saved:** 0 hours (because you spent 5 hours setting it up).
*   **Result:** You just burned $30 and half a day of development time for a feature that didn't ship.

I’ve wasted hundreds of dollars on "game-changing" AI agents that couldn't even handle a simple database schema. I broke my production environment twice trying to force an AI agent to write my migrations. Don't be me. Focus on the product, not the hype cycle. If your tool doesn't move the needle, delete it. If it doesn't make you money, it’s just a toy.
