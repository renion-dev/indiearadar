If you’re pricing your AI features based on "market value" rather than your actual API costs, you’re just paying for the privilege of working for free.

> **⚡ TL;DR**
> *   **Subscriptions** are for steady cash flow, but they kill your margins if your users are AI power-users.
> *   **Usage-based** billing is the only way to protect your sanity when LLM token prices fluctuate.
> *   **One-time payments** are a trap unless you’re selling a static, non-API-dependent tool. 
> *   **Skip this if:** You have venture capital backing and don't care about profitability for the next three years.

## 🧠 The Reality Check
Everyone says "subscription models are king." That’s a lie when you’re building on top of OpenAI or Anthropic. If you charge $20/month for "unlimited" AI access, one power user will run a script, hit your API 50,000 times, and bankrupt your margins in a weekend. I learned this the hard way when I left an agent running and woke up to a $400 bill. Stop treating AI like a static SaaS feature.

## ⚙️ The Solopreneur Playbook
1.  **Calculate your base cost:** Determine the average cost of tokens per user action, then add a 30% buffer for margin.
2.  **Pick your lane:** Use subscriptions for the "platform access" and usage-based credits for the heavy AI compute.
3.  **Set hard caps:** Never offer "unlimited" anything; implement a credit system so you can cut off users before they bleed your wallet dry.
4.  **Automate your alerts:** Use tools like Helicone or LangSmith to ping you when your daily API spend crosses a specific threshold.

## 📉 The Catch (aka The Fine Print)
Usage-based billing is an accounting nightmare. Customers hate not knowing their monthly bill, and you will spend half your life dealing with "why am I out of credits" support tickets. It’s a friction-heavy model that can kill your conversion rates if the user experience isn't seamless.

**The Builders' Math**
*   API Cost: $0.02 per query.
*   Your Price: $0.10 per query (5x markup).
*   User usage: 100 queries/month.
*   Revenue: $10.00. 
*   Profit: $8.00.
*   *Verdict:* At 100 users, you’re clearing $800/mo profit without worrying about a "whale" user bankrupting your business.

Stop chasing the "all-you-can-eat" model. It works for Netflix, not for someone paying $0.0001 per token. If you can’t track your cost per click, you’re not a founder—you’re a gambler.
