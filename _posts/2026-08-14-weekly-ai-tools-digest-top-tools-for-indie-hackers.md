# 📈 Weekly Insight
**The Rise of "Agentic" Workflows**
The trend this week has shifted from "chatbots" to "agents"—AI that performs multi-step actions rather than just generating text. The release of Claude’s computer-use capabilities marks a transition where AI is moving from a consultant to a digital employee.

For indie founders, this is a massive shift. You no longer need to pay for expensive integration platforms (like Zapier) for every single task. You can now prompt an agent to open a browser, log into your Stripe dashboard, export a CSV, and email it to your accountant. Focus on identifying your most tedious, high-frequency manual task this week; it is likely now automatable for pennies via an agent. Stop building "chat" interfaces and start building "action" loops.
