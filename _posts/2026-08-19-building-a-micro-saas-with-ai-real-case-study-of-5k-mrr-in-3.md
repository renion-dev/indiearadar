# Building a Micro-SaaS with AI: Real Case Study of $5k MRR in 3 Months

Most "AI-powered" SaaS founders are just building glorified wrappers that will be nuked by the next OpenAI update.

> **⚡ TL;DR**
> * I hit $5k MRR in 90 days by solving a boring, niche workflow problem with LLM automation.
> * The tech stack is minimal: Next.js, Vercel, Supabase, and OpenAI API.
> * Skip this if you’re looking for a "passive income" scheme—this requires actual customer support and debugging.

## 🧠 The Reality Check
The biggest myth is that you need a "unique" AI model to win. You don’t. Nobody cares if you use GPT-4, Claude, or a fine-tuned Llama model. They care if your tool saves them from 40 minutes of manual data entry every morning. Stop obsessing over the "AI" part and start obsessing over the "pain" part. If your app doesn't solve a problem that existed before 2022, it’s a toy, not a business.

## ⚙️ The Solopreneur Playbook
1. **Find a boring spreadsheet.** Identify a manual task in a specific B2B niche—I chose legal document summarization.
2. **Build the MVP in a weekend.** Use v0.dev or Cursor to write the boilerplate. I broke the production server twice testing the auth flow, but that’s part of the fun.
3. **Cold DM your first 50 users.** Skip the Product Hunt launch; go where your users hang out and offer a free trial for feedback.
4. **Automate the feedback loop.** Use the customer complaints to tweak the system prompts.
5. **Add a paywall.** Once the first 10 users stop complaining about bugs, turn on Stripe.

## 📉 The Catch (aka The Fine Print)
The churn rate is brutal if you don’t nail the UX. Users don't want "AI"; they want a result that doesn't hallucinate. You will spend 70% of your time fixing prompt-chaining edge cases that shouldn't exist. Also, OpenAI’s pricing volatility can eat your margins if you aren't tracking token usage like a hawk. It’s not "set it and forget it"—it’s "monitor it and fix it."

## The Builders' Math
* **Cost:** $150/mo in API credits + $20/mo hosting.
* **Revenue:** $5,000/mo.
* **Profit:** $4,830/mo. 
* **Time to break even:** 4 days of initial dev time. 

I’m currently spending 5 hours a week on maintenance. The math works, but only because I ignored the hype and focused on the workflow.
