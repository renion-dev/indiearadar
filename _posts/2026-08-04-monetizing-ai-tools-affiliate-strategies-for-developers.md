Most AI affiliate programs are glorified pyramid schemes for influencers with zero technical depth.

> **⚡ TL;DR**
> * High-ticket B2B AI software offers better commissions than cheap consumer wrappers.
> * Build a "Tools I Actually Use" page rather than spamming referral links on Twitter.
> * Skip this if you don’t have an existing audience or a specific niche problem you’ve already solved.

## 🧠 The Reality Check
The biggest myth is that you can get rich by link-stuffing your bio with every AI wrapper that launches on Product Hunt. You can’t. If you promote junk, your audience stops trusting you, and your conversion rate drops to zero. Real monetization comes from being a curator, not a salesperson. If you aren't using the tool in your own build process, your recommendation smells like desperation.

## ⚙️ The Solopreneur Playbook
1. **Curate for your stack:** Only join affiliate programs for tools that fit your specific tech niche (e.g., Cursor, Vercel, or specialized APIs).
2. **Document the build:** Record a 60-second Loom video showing how the tool solved a specific bug in your current project.
3. **Embed, don't blast:** Place the link directly inside a "Tech Stack" page on your portfolio or a README file.
4. **Contextual triggers:** Only share the link when someone asks, "How did you build that feature?" on Discord or X.

## 📉 The Catch
Most AI affiliate programs have a 30-day cookie window. If your customer takes two months to decide, you get nothing. Worse, many SaaS companies change their terms mid-stream, slashing commissions just as you start gaining traction. I once spent a weekend writing a tutorial for a tool that cut its affiliate payouts by 50% on Monday morning. Yes, I was annoyed.

**The Builders' Math**
Let's say you promote a developer-focused AI tool at $49/mo with a 20% recurring commission.
*   **Commission:** $9.80/mo per user.
*   **Goal:** 10 signups.
*   **Result:** $98/mo in passive recurring revenue.
*   **Effort:** 3 hours of content creation.
*   **Payoff:** It breaks even on your time in month two.

If you’re looking for a get-rich-quick scheme, go find a gambling affiliate program. If you want to subsidize your server costs by sharing tools that actually make your life easier, this is the only way to do it without losing your soul.
