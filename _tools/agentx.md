## What is AgentX?

**AgentX** is an all-in-one diagnostic and optimization platform designed for developers building AI agents. Its core mission is to remove the guesswork from AI performance by allowing you to evaluate your agents, pinpoint specific failure points, and deploy fixes with a single click. By integrating seamlessly with your existing tech stack, AgentX acts as a "quality assurance engineer" for your LLM workflows.

## Key Features

*   **Comprehensive Evaluation:** Rigorously test your agents against custom datasets to measure accuracy, hallucination rates, and latency.
*   **Root Cause Analysis:** Go beyond surface-level metrics. AgentX traces your agent’s decision-making process to show you exactly where the logic breaks down.
*   **One-Click Remediation:** Don’t just identify the problem; fix it. The platform allows you to apply prompt adjustments or configuration patches instantly.
*   **MCP Integration:** Built with the Model Context Protocol (MCP) in mind, it ensures your agents can securely and efficiently access the data they need to perform optimally.
*   **Real-time Monitoring:** Keep a pulse on how your agent performs in production, ensuring it continues to meet quality standards as users interact with it.
