## What is NINA?
NINA is an AI-powered interactive onboarding platform that guides users step-by-step through your software interface. It replaces static documentation by creating contextual, in-app walkthroughs that help new users reach their "Aha!" moment faster without leaving your product.

## Key Features
- **No-Code Walkthrough Builder:** Create complex user flows and interactive tours without writing a single line of code.
- **Contextual AI Assistance:** NINA understands user intent and provides specific guidance based on where they are stuck in your UI.
- **Analytics Dashboard:** Track where users drop off in your onboarding flow to identify friction points in your product design.
- **Multi-Language Support:** Automatically localize your guides to provide a seamless experience for a global user base.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
For indie hackers, time is the most valuable resource. NINA is a game-changer because it eliminates the need to constantly update help center articles or record new Loom videos every time you push an UI update. By embedding the guidance directly into the product, developers can reduce support tickets significantly and boost activation rates for their MVP. It’s the closest thing to having a dedicated customer success agent for every single user, allowing the founder to focus on building features rather than answering repetitive "how-to" questions.

## Verdict
**Best for:** SaaS founders and product managers looking to improve user retention and minimize onboarding friction.
**Skip if:** You have a very simple, single-page application or a static website that doesn't require complex user education.
