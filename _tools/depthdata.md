## What is DepthData?
DepthData is a centralized financial intelligence platform designed to track, manage, and optimize your company’s AI infrastructure costs. By acting as the "system of record" for your AI spend, it provides granular visibility into token usage, API calls, and model performance across your entire stack.

## Key Features
- **Unified Dashboard:** Consolidate spend data from OpenAI, Anthropic, Cohere, and other providers into a single, real-time view.
- **Granular Attribution:** Tag API costs by project, team, or specific feature to understand exactly where your budget is going.
- **Usage Anomaly Alerts:** Set automated triggers to notify your team when spend spikes or usage patterns deviate from the norm.
- **Model Efficiency Benchmarking:** Compare the cost-to-performance ratio of different models to ensure you’re using the most cost-effective solution for your specific tasks.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
For indie hackers and lean teams, "AI bloat" is a real danger—one runaway script or poorly optimized prompt loop can lead to an unexpected four-figure bill. DepthData is a favorite among the community because it removes the guesswork from scaling. By providing clear visibility into which specific features or API keys are driving costs, developers can make data-backed decisions on whether to switch to a cheaper model or optimize their prompt engineering, ensuring their burn rate stays sustainable while they chase product-market fit.

## Verdict
**Best for:** CTOs, solo founders, and engineering leads who are scaling AI-integrated applications and need strict cost control.

**Skip if:** You are only making occasional API calls for testing purposes or your monthly AI spend is negligible enough that manual tracking in a spreadsheet suffices.
