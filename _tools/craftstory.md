## What is CraftStory?
CraftStory is an AI-powered video creation platform that specializes in generating photorealistic human avatars for content production. By utilizing compact AI models, it allows users to convert text into lifelike video presentations without the need for expensive cameras or studio setups.

## Key Features
*   **Photorealistic Avatars:** Generates high-fidelity human digital doubles that mimic natural speech and expressions.
*   **Compact AI Architecture:** Optimized for faster rendering times and lower latency compared to bulky, cloud-heavy competitors.
*   **Text-to-Video Engine:** Seamlessly turns scripts into professional-grade narrations with synchronized lip-movement.
*   **Multi-Language Support:** Automatically adjusts avatar mouth movements to match various languages, perfect for global content scaling.
