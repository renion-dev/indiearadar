## What is mectrics?
Mectrics is a lightweight, open-source utility designed to place your Mac’s essential system vitals directly into your menu bar. It provides real-time monitoring of hardware performance without the bloat of resource-heavy system management suites.

## Key Features
- **Real-time Monitoring:** Track CPU usage, memory pressure, and battery health at a glance.
- **Minimalist Design:** A clean, unobtrusive interface that blends seamlessly with macOS aesthetics.
- **Open Source:** Fully transparent codebase, allowing for community audits and custom modifications.
- **Low Resource Footprint:** Optimized to ensure the tool itself doesn’t become a drain on your system’s performance.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers and power users gravitate toward mectrics because it solves the "bloatware problem" common in system monitoring apps. When you are running heavy IDEs, Docker containers, or video editing software, you need to know exactly why your fans are spinning up without sacrificing 5% of your CPU just to monitor it. Because it is open-source and privacy-focused, developers trust it to run in the background 24/7 without worrying about telemetry or hidden data collection.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Developers, power users, and minimalists who want granular control over their Mac's performance data without the fluff or subscription fees.

**Skip if:** You require advanced features like fan speed control, temperature logging, or deep-dive diagnostic reporting, which are typically found in paid alternatives like iStat Menus.
