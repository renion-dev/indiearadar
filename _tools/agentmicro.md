## What is AgentMicro?
AgentMicro is a lightweight macOS menu bar utility designed to provide real-time monitoring of your Codex AI task statuses. It bridges the gap between your background AI processes and your desktop environment, ensuring you stay updated without needing to toggle between browser tabs or code editors.

## Key Features
- **Live Status Feed:** View the real-time progress of your active AI agents directly from your menu bar.
- **Instant Notifications:** Receive push alerts the moment a task is completed, fails, or requires human intervention.
- **Quick-Action Controls:** Pause, restart, or cancel ongoing AI tasks with a single click.
- **Minimalist UI:** A clean, non-intrusive interface that stays out of your way until you need it.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers often juggle multiple AI agents for coding, research, and content generation simultaneously. AgentMicro is a game-changer because it eliminates "tab fatigue"—the constant context switching required to check if an agent has finished its job. By offloading the status monitoring to the macOS menu bar, developers can maintain their flow state in their IDE while keeping a passive eye on their background automation, making it an essential tool for solo founders who need to maximize their efficiency per hour.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Solo developers and indie makers who rely heavily on AI-assisted coding workflows and want to streamline their desktop experience.

**Skip if:** You don’t use Codex-based AI tools or prefer a strictly browser-based workflow where you don’t mind checking status updates manually.
