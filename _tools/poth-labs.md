## What is Poth Labs?
Poth Labs acts as the "customer brain" for your company by aggregating fragmented customer feedback, support tickets, and sales calls into a single, searchable intelligence layer. It uses AI to synthesize these qualitative data points into actionable insights, helping teams make product decisions based on what users are actually saying rather than guesswork.

## Key Features
- **Unified Data Ingestion:** Automatically pulls feedback from Slack, email, Zendesk, and Intercom into one centralized hub.
- **AI-Powered Synthesis:** Identifies recurring pain points, feature requests, and sentiment trends without manual tagging.
- **Smart Search:** Allows you to ask natural language questions like "What are the top reasons users churned last month?" and get evidence-backed answers.
- **Product Roadmap Alignment:** Connects specific customer feedback directly to your product backlog, ensuring development remains customer-centric.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
For the solo founder or small team, the biggest bottleneck is often "context switching" between support tickets and product development. Indie hackers love Poth Labs because it replaces hours of manual spreadsheet tracking with an automated "second brain." When you're building in public and iterating fast, having a tool that instantly surfaces why a user is frustrated—and links it to a specific feature—saves massive amounts of development time and prevents building features that nobody actually asked for.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Product managers, solo founders, and customer success teams at SaaS companies who are drowning in feedback and need a way to prioritize their roadmap objectively.

**Skip if:** You are a very early-stage project with less than 50 customer interactions per month; the overhead of setting up integrations might outweigh the value until you hit a higher volume of feedback.
