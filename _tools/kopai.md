## What is Kopai?
Kopai is an AI-powered platform that allows you to transform your personal expertise into autonomous AI agents. By uploading your knowledge base, you can deploy agents that interact with your audience, solve problems, and generate passive revenue on your behalf.

## Key Features
- **Knowledge Base Integration:** Easily upload your documents, URLs, and past content to train your agent on your specific tone and expertise.
- **Autonomous Monetization:** Configure your agents to charge for their consultations or services, turning your brainpower into a 24/7 income stream.
- **Customizable AI Personas:** Tailor your agent’s personality and problem-solving logic to match your brand or professional style.
- **Seamless Embedding:** Integrate your agents directly into your website or landing pages to capture leads and resolve queries without human intervention.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers are flocking to Kopai because it solves the "trading time for money" bottleneck. Instead of manually answering the same consulting questions or support emails, a solo founder can create a Kopai agent that acts as a digital twin. For example, a niche SEO consultant can train an agent on their specific methodology and offer it as a subscription-based "Audit Bot" to their clients. It allows creators to capture value from their expertise even while they sleep, effectively decoupling income from active work hours.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Solopreneurs, consultants, and content creators who want to productize their knowledge and automate client interactions.

**Skip if:** You don’t have a proprietary knowledge base or specific expertise to train an agent on, or if you prefer high-touch, human-only interactions for your business model.
