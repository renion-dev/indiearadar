## What is Clera?

**Clera** is an intelligent AI agent designed to bridge the gap between talent and opportunity. By acting as a sophisticated matchmaker, it automates the tedious parts of the recruitment process, ensuring that candidates are not just qualified on paper, but are the "right fit" for specific roles. Whether you are a recruiter looking to streamline your pipeline or a company aiming to reduce hiring bias, Clera acts as a tireless digital partner that understands the nuances of professional skill sets.

## Key Features

*   **Smart Matching Engine:** Clera goes beyond keyword matching. It analyzes candidate profiles, portfolios, and experience to determine genuine role compatibility.
*   **Automated Screening:** It handles the heavy lifting of initial resume reviews, surfacing top-tier talent so you can focus on high-impact interviews.
*   **Bias-Aware Filtering:** Designed to focus on merit, Clera helps remove unconscious bias from the early stages of the hiring funnel.
*   **Seamless Integration:** It plugs into existing workflows, ensuring you don’t have to abandon your current tech stack to get better results.
