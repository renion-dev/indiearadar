## What is StoreClaw?

**StoreClaw** (https://www.storeclaw.ai) is an AI-powered growth platform designed specifically for e-commerce store owners. Its primary mission is to turn passive browsers into paying customers by deploying intelligent, autonomous agents that understand the nuances of sales. Unlike generic chatbots that simply answer FAQs, StoreClaw’s agents are trained to actively persuade, recommend products, and handle objections to boost your conversion rates and average order value.

## Key Features

*   **Sales-Driven AI Agents:** These aren't just support bots; they are trained to "sell." They proactively engage visitors, offer personalized product recommendations, and guide them through the checkout process.
*   **Seamless E-commerce Integration:** StoreClaw connects directly to your store’s inventory, ensuring the AI has real-time data on stock, pricing, and product details.
*   **Behavioral Targeting:** The agents trigger based on specific visitor actions, such as cart abandonment or browsing history, allowing for perfectly timed interventions.
*   **Actionable Analytics:** Get deep insights into how your AI agents are impacting your bottom line, including direct revenue attribution.

## Pricing

StoreClaw offers a tiered pricing structure designed to scale with your business. While they offer a free trial to test the efficacy of their agents, their paid plans are based on "conversations" or "sales assisted." This ensures that you are paying for actual value delivered rather than just software overhead. Detailed pricing can be found on their website.
