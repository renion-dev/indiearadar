## What is Osaurus?

**Osaurus** (https://osaurus.ai) is a powerful desktop application designed to bring the power of autonomous AI agents directly to your Mac. Unlike cloud-based alternatives that require API keys and external subscriptions, Osaurus focuses on privacy and local execution. It enables users to deploy open-source LLMs that run entirely on their machine’s hardware, ensuring your data never leaves your device.

## Key Features

*   **100% Local Execution:** No cloud dependencies, no data tracking, and zero latency issues caused by server traffic.
*   **Open Source Compatibility:** Easily swap between various open-source models (like Llama 3 or Mistral) to find the best fit for your specific tasks.
*   **Autonomous Agent Workflow:** Osaurus doesn't just chat; it executes tasks. You can define goals, and the agent navigates, processes, and completes them locally.
*   **Privacy-First Architecture:** Because everything runs on your Mac, your sensitive documents, codebases, and conversations remain completely private and offline.
*   **Native Mac Integration:** Built specifically for macOS, the app leverages Apple Silicon for optimized performance and energy efficiency.

## Pricing

Osaurus operates on a transparent model. The core tool is free to download and use locally. For power users, they offer a Pro tier that unlocks advanced agent orchestration features, priority updates, and professional support. Given the savings on recurring API costs (like OpenAI or Anthropic), the ROI for heavy users is immediate.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It

Indie hackers and developers are flocking to Osaurus for three main reasons:
1.  **Cost Efficiency:** No more "token fatigue" or monthly billing surprises. Once you have the hardware, the usage is essentially free.
2.  **Security:** When working on proprietary code or sensitive startup data, sending information to the cloud is a non-starter. Osaurus allows for rapid prototyping without the security risk.
3.  **Offline Capability:** Whether you are working from a coffee shop with spotty Wi-Fi or traveling, your AI agent is always ready to work.

Osaurus is a game-changer for Mac users who want the benefits of AI agents without the privacy trade-offs or recurring costs of SaaS platforms. If you have an Apple Silicon Mac and want a private, autonomous, and lightning-fast AI workflow, **Osaurus is a must-install.**
