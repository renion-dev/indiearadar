# Review: Cursor for iOS

**Tagline: Build with coding agents from anywhere**
**Website:** [https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cursor/id6767085653](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cursor/id6767085653)

## What is Cursor for iOS?
Cursor for iOS is the mobile extension of the industry-leading AI code editor. It brings the power of intelligent coding agents to your iPhone, allowing you to review code, troubleshoot bugs, and brainstorm architecture while away from your desk. It isn’t just a viewer; it is a companion designed to keep your development momentum going when you’re on the move.

## Key Features
*   **AI-Powered Chat:** Access the same powerful LLMs found in the desktop version to debug snippets or explain complex logic on the fly.
*   **Context-Aware Sync:** Seamlessly sync your projects and repositories, ensuring that the AI understands your specific codebase, not just generic programming syntax.
*   **Mobile-Optimized Interface:** A clean, touch-friendly UI that makes reading and editing code intuitive, even on a smaller screen.
*   **Real-time Agentic Support:** Leverage coding agents that can propose changes and suggest improvements directly within your mobile session.

## Pricing
Cursor for iOS follows the standard Cursor pricing model. It offers a generous **Free tier** for hobbyists and individual developers, while the **Pro tier** ($20/month) unlocks unlimited advanced model usage, higher rate limits, and priority support. Since your subscription is tied to your account, your desktop Pro benefits translate perfectly to your mobile experience.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
For the solo founder or indie hacker, time is the ultimate currency. Cursor for iOS eliminates the "dead time" during commutes or travel. Whether you’re fixing a production bug while at a coffee shop or drafting a new feature implementation while waiting for a flight, this app ensures you’re never truly disconnected from your project. It turns your iPhone into a legitimate terminal for productivity, allowing you to push changes and stay ahead of your roadmap.

## Verdict
Cursor for iOS is an essential utility for any developer who values flexibility. While you likely won’t be refactoring your entire backend on a phone, the ability to consult with an AI agent about your actual code—wherever you are—is a game-changer. It is polished, fast, and brings the future of AI-assisted development right into your pocket. 

**Rating: 4.8/5**
