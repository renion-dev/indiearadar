## What is Glaze by Raycast?

**Glaze** is a revolutionary tool that turns the complex world of macOS app development into a simple conversation. Built by the team behind the productivity powerhouse Raycast, Glaze allows you to build, customize, and deploy functional Mac applications using natural language. Whether you are a non-technical founder or a seasoned developer looking to prototype rapidly, Glaze acts as an AI-powered co-pilot that writes the code, handles the UI, and manages the build process for you.

## Key Features

*   **Natural Language Coding:** Describe the app you want in plain English, and Glaze generates the underlying Swift code.
*   **Instant Previews:** See your app take shape in real-time as you refine prompts.
*   **Raycast Integration:** Leverage the ecosystem of Raycast to create powerful extensions and standalone tools that feel native to macOS.
*   **One-Click Deployment:** Glaze handles the heavy lifting of packaging and signing, making it easier than ever to ship your app to users.
*   **Iterative Refinement:** Easily tweak features, change UI elements, or add functionality by simply chatting with the AI.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It

Indie hackers are flocking to Glaze because it removes the "blank page" syndrome and the steep learning curve of Xcode. It allows solo creators to bridge the gap between an idea and a functional `.app` file in hours rather than weeks. By abstracting away the boilerplate code and complex Apple documentation, Glaze empowers developers to focus on product-market fit and user experience rather than syntax errors.
