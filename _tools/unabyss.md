## What is Unabyss?

**Unabyss** is an MCP-native (Model Context Protocol) infrastructure tool designed to solve the "context window" problem for AI agents. Rather than relying on static RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines that quickly become stale, Unabyss acts as a dynamic, self-updating context layer. It bridges the gap between your fragmented data sources and your AI agents, ensuring they always have access to the most relevant, real-time information without manual intervention.

## Key Features

*   **MCP-Native Integration:** Built from the ground up for the Model Context Protocol, allowing seamless "plug-and-play" connectivity with any MCP-compliant AI client (like Claude Desktop or Cursor).
*   **Self-Updating Context:** Unabyss monitors your data sources—be it GitHub repositories, Notion docs, or internal APIs—and automatically refreshes the context layer. No more "stale data" hallucinations.
*   **Intelligent Prioritization:** It doesn’t just dump data into your prompt; it curates the most impactful context based on the current task, optimizing token usage and model performance.
*   **Zero-Config Sync:** Connect your tech stack in minutes, not hours. The platform handles the indexing and synchronization logic under the hood.
