## What is Mailwarm 2.0?

**Mailwarm 2.0** is the latest iteration of the industry-standard email warmup service designed to keep your outreach out of the spam folder. By leveraging a sophisticated network of real human-like accounts, it automatically sends, opens, and replies to emails from your inbox. This process "teaches" email service providers (ESPs) like Google and Outlook that your domain is trustworthy, ultimately boosting your sender reputation and ensuring your cold emails land exactly where they belong: the primary inbox.

## Key Features

*   **Smart Warmup Sequences:** Unlike static bots, Mailwarm 2.0 mimics natural human behavior with randomized sending patterns and intelligent interaction logic.
*   **Deliverability Dashboard:** Gain deep insights into your current domain health, spam scores, and inbox placement rates with easy-to-read analytics.
*   **Automatic Spam Recovery:** If your emails start hitting the junk folder, the system automatically adjusts your warmup strategy to repair your reputation.
*   **Seamless Integrations:** Compatible with all major ESPs (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Zoho) and easily plugs into popular cold email platforms like Instantly or Lemlist.
