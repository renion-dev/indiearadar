## What is own.page?
**own.page** is a streamlined website builder designed for creators, developers, and entrepreneurs who want a clean, aesthetic, and highly functional personal landing page. Eschewing the complexity of traditional builders like WordPress or Webflow, own.page utilizes a "bento box" grid system. This modular approach allows you to stack links, social profiles, projects, and custom content into a visually cohesive, modern layout that feels professional and intentional.

## Key Features
*   **Bento Grid Interface:** Effortlessly drag and drop content into a grid that looks great on both desktop and mobile.
*   **Customization:** Tailor your site with custom colors, fonts, and tile styles to match your personal brand.
*   **Rich Integrations:** Easily embed content from GitHub, Twitter, Spotify, or your latest blog posts directly into tiles.
*   **Performance Focused:** The platform is optimized for speed and SEO, ensuring your personal portfolio ranks well and loads instantly.
*   **No-Code Simplicity:** You don’t need to write a single line of HTML or CSS to achieve a high-end design.

## Pricing
own.page offers a **Freemium model**. You can get started for free to test the waters. For power users, they offer a Pro tier that unlocks custom domains, advanced analytics, and deeper customization options. It is competitively priced for the value it provides, making it an affordable "set it and forget it" solution for your digital presence.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers and solo founders gravitate toward own.page because it solves the "personal site dilemma." It eliminates the time-sink of maintaining a complex website while still providing a space that looks sophisticated. It’s perfect for showcasing side projects, newsletter sign-ups, and professional bios without the bloat of traditional CMS platforms. It’s the ultimate tool for those who want to "ship" their personal brand in under an hour.

If you are looking for a high-signal, low-maintenance way to present yourself online, **own.page is a winner**. It masterfully balances aesthetic appeal with functional design.
