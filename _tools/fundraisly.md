## What is Fundraisly?

Fundraisly is an AI-powered fundraising agent designed to bridge the gap between ambitious founders and venture capital. Instead of spending weeks manually scouring LinkedIn or Crunchbase, Fundraisly automates the entire top-of-funnel process. It acts as an autonomous outbound sales rep for your startup, identifying relevant investors and handling the outreach until a meeting is secured on your calendar.

## Key Features

*   **Smart Investor Matching:** The platform uses AI to analyze your startup’s industry, stage, and geography to build a curated list of investors who actually invest in your specific niche.
*   **Automated Outreach:** Fundraisly drafts personalized, high-conversion emails that sound human, not robotic, significantly increasing your chances of getting a response.
*   **Meeting Scheduling:** The tool manages the back-and-forth scheduling process, ensuring that when an investor expresses interest, a meeting is booked directly onto your calendar without the administrative overhead.
*   **Performance Tracking:** A clean dashboard allows you to monitor open rates, reply rates, and active conversations in real-time.

## Pricing

Fundraisly offers tiered pricing designed to scale with your fundraising round. While they offer a free trial to test the quality of their investor database, their paid plans typically operate on a subscription model. Founders can choose between monthly "sprint" plans—ideal for active fundraising rounds—or annual options for those continuously building investor relationships. Detailed pricing is available on their website at [fundraisly.com](https://fundraisly.com/).

## Why Indie Hackers Love It

For indie hackers and solo founders, time is the scarcest resource. Fundraising is often described as a "full-time job" that distracts from product development. Fundraisly is a favorite in the community because it allows founders to "outsource" the grunt work of networking. It turns the daunting task of cold outreach into a set-it-and-forget-it workflow, allowing founders to stay focused on shipping code and growing revenue while the AI builds the cap table.
