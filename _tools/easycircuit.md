## What is EasyCircuit?
EasyCircuit is an AI-powered platform that accelerates hardware prototyping by allowing users to generate schematics and PCB designs using natural language prompts. It bridges the gap between conceptual ideas and manufacturing-ready files, effectively bringing the "vibe-coding" paradigm to electrical engineering.

## Key Features
- **Natural Language to Schematic:** Simply describe your circuit requirements, and the AI generates the corresponding schematic in seconds.
- **Automated Component Selection:** Suggests industry-standard, available components to ensure your design is actually manufacturable.
- **Live DRC (Design Rule Check):** Real-time error detection that catches connectivity issues before you waste money on physical prototypes.
- **One-Click Export:** Seamlessly push designs to Gerber files or integrated manufacturing partners.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers are flocking to EasyCircuit because it removes the steep learning curve of traditional EDA software like Altium or KiCad. For a solo founder trying to bring a custom IoT gadget or sensor array to market, the ability to "describe" a power management circuit or a microcontroller interface—rather than spending hours manually routing traces—is a massive force multiplier. It turns a weeks-long hardware design phase into a weekend project, allowing makers to focus on the software integration and product vision.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Software engineers transitioning into hardware, rapid prototypers, and indie founders who need to get a PCB design to the factory floor without hiring an electrical engineer.

**Skip if:** You are designing complex, high-speed, or high-voltage multi-layer boards that require granular, manual control over every trace and signal integrity simulation. EasyCircuit is for velocity, not high-end industrial precision.
