## What is Basedash Audit Logs?
Basedash Audit Logs is a comprehensive observability tool that tracks every interaction within your internal BI and database management interface. It provides a transparent, immutable record of who performed which action, ensuring total accountability and security for your data operations.

## Key Features
- **Granular Activity Tracking:** Log every query execution, data export, and configuration change made by your team members in real-time.
- **Searchable History:** Quickly filter through logs using intuitive parameters like user, action type, or timestamp to pinpoint specific incidents.
- **Security & Compliance:** Maintain a robust audit trail required for SOC2 compliance and internal data governance standards.
- **Alerting & Monitoring:** Receive notifications for suspicious activities or unauthorized attempts to access sensitive database tables.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
For indie hackers and solo founders scaling their SaaS, Basedash Audit Logs acts as an essential "safety net." When you grant team members or contractors access to your production database, the fear of accidental deletions or unauthorized data leaks is real. Indie hackers love this tool because it requires zero configuration to start monitoring; it turns a "black box" environment into a transparent workspace, allowing founders to delegate database tasks without losing sleep over the "who, what, and when" of their data integrity.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Founders and engineering teams who need to maintain strict data security and oversight as they scale their internal operations.

**Skip if:** You are a solo developer working on a project with no external contributors or sensitive user data, where overhead of tracking might be unnecessary.
