## What is Fathom 3.0?

Fathom 3.0 is a sophisticated AI-powered meeting assistant designed to eliminate the manual labor of note-taking. Unlike traditional tools that clutter your video calls with intrusive "AI bots," Fathom 3.0 offers a seamless, discreet experience. It captures, transcribes, and summarizes your Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams calls, then integrates directly into your existing workflow—including ChatGPT and Claude—to turn raw conversation into actionable intelligence.

## Key Features

*   **Bot-Free Experience:** No more awkward "AI bot has joined the meeting" notifications. Fathom operates in the background, maintaining professional decorum.
*   **Deep LLM Integration:** You can push meeting transcripts directly into ChatGPT or Claude. This allows you to ask nuanced questions about your calls, such as "Draft a project roadmap based on this client discussion" or "What were the main objections raised?"
*   **Action Item Sync:** Automatically extracts tasks and pushes them to project management tools like Notion, Slack, Jira, and Asana.
*   **Multi-Language Support:** High-accuracy transcription across dozens of languages.
*   **Smart Summaries:** Generates concise, structured recaps that capture the "who, what, and why" of every meeting.
