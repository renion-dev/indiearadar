## What is Prompt Anything: No more AI slop?
Prompt Anything is an intelligent prompt-engineering assistant designed to bridge the gap between mediocre AI outputs and professional-grade results. It helps users transform vague ideas into structured, high-context prompts that eliminate the "slop" often associated with generic AI responses.

## Key Features
*   **Context Optimization:** Automatically refines your input to provide AI models with the necessary constraints, persona, and output format.
*   **Library of Frameworks:** Access proven prompt engineering methodologies (like Chain-of-Thought or Few-Shot prompting) without needing to be an expert.
*   **Model-Agnostic:** Works seamlessly whether you are using ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or local LLMs.
*   **Iterative Feedback Loop:** Built-in tools to refine prompts based on AI performance, ensuring continuous improvement of your outputs.
