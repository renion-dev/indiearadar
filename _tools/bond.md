## What is Bond?
**Bond** (https://www.bondapp.io/) is an intelligent task management platform that aims to solve the "to-do list graveyard" problem. Unlike traditional apps that simply store your tasks, Bond acts as an autonomous assistant. By integrating with your calendar, email, and workflow tools, it doesn't just remind you to work—it helps you prioritize, schedule, and execute your tasks automatically. Its tagline, "The AI to-do list that does itself," captures its mission to minimize manual planning and maximize deep work.

## Key Features
*   **Smart Scheduling:** Bond analyzes your workload and automatically slots tasks into your calendar during your most productive hours.
*   **Context-Aware Automation:** It integrates with your existing tech stack, pulling action items directly from emails and Slack so you never miss a follow-up.
*   **Adaptive Prioritization:** The AI learns your habits and deadlines, re-shuffling your daily agenda in real-time if a meeting runs over or a priority shifts.
*   **Natural Language Input:** You can "brain dump" tasks into the app, and Bond will parse them into structured, actionable items with deadlines and categories.

## Pricing
Bond offers a tiered subscription model:
*   **Free:** Ideal for individuals testing the waters, offering core AI scheduling and basic integrations.
*   **Pro:** A monthly or annual subscription that unlocks unlimited integrations, advanced AI priority modeling, and team collaboration features.
*   **Enterprise:** Custom solutions for larger organizations needing advanced security and API access.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers and solo founders gravitate toward Bond because it eliminates the "meta-work" of productivity. When you are a team of one, every minute spent organizing a list is a minute taken away from shipping code or talking to customers. Bond acts as an automated project manager, allowing creators to focus on execution while the AI handles the administrative overhead of keeping a schedule balanced.

## Verdict
Bond is a game-changer for those overwhelmed by traditional, static to-do lists. It successfully bridges the gap between a notepad and a project manager. While it requires a bit of trust to let an AI "auto-schedule" your life, the time saved and the reduction in decision fatigue make it an essential tool for the modern, high-output professional.
