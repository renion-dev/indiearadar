## What is unquestion?
**unquestion** is an AI-powered form builder that replaces static, boring questionnaires with dynamic, conversational interfaces. By leveraging LLMs, it turns data collection into a natural dialogue, adapting its follow-up questions based on the user's previous answers.

## Key Features
- **Conversational AI Flow:** Instead of a rigid grid, the form asks follow-up questions in real-time, making the experience feel like a one-on-one chat.
- **Smart Data Extraction:** Automatically parses unstructured user responses into clean, organized data fields.
- **Dynamic Branching:** Eliminates "dead-end" paths by adjusting the questioning strategy based on the context of the conversation.
- **Seamless Integrations:** Connects easily with existing CRMs and databases to ensure the conversational data doesn't get stuck in a silo.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers are using unquestion to slash their bounce rates on lead generation and user onboarding. Because the form "thinks" like a person, users are far more likely to complete complex surveys or qualifying questions that would normally cause them to close a browser tab. For a solo founder, it’s like having a dedicated SDR on staff 24/7, turning passive form submissions into high-quality, actionable insights without the manual follow-up work.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Founders, marketers, and researchers who need high completion rates for complex surveys or lead qualification forms.
