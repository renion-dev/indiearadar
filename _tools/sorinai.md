## What is Sorinai?
Sorinai is an interactive AI-powered notepad designed to transform how you handle meeting notes. By integrating directly into your workflow, it captures, summarizes, and allows you to chat with your meeting transcripts to extract actionable insights instantly.

## Key Features
*   **Smart Meeting Transcription:** Automatically captures high-fidelity audio and converts it into structured, searchable text.
*   **Interactive "Chat with Notes":** Ask specific questions about your meetings—like "What were the agreed-upon deadlines?"—and get instant, context-aware answers.
*   **Action Item Extraction:** Automatically identifies and highlights tasks, owners, and due dates so nothing slips through the cracks.
*   **Seamless Integrations:** Syncs with your existing calendar and video conferencing tools to ensure your workflow remains uninterrupted.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers and solo founders gravitate toward Sorinai because it acts as a force multiplier for productivity. When you are wearing every hat in a business, you don't have time to manually transcribe discovery calls or hunt through hour-long recordings to find a specific client requirement. Sorinai allows founders to stay present during sales calls or brainstorming sessions, knowing the "heavy lifting" of documentation is being handled in the background. It turns a chaotic transcript into a clean, actionable database, which is essential for maintaining momentum in a lean startup environment.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Founders, freelancers, and remote team leads who spend too much time manually documenting meetings and need to turn verbal conversations into structured data quickly.

**Skip if:** You work in highly regulated industries that forbid third-party AI recording tools, or if you prefer the tactile, manual process of taking handwritten notes to help you process information.
