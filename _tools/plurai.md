## What is Plurai?

Plurai is a specialized evaluation and guardrails platform designed to help developers move beyond generic LLM benchmarks. Instead of relying on one-size-fits-all metrics, Plurai focuses on "vibe-training"—a process that aligns AI behavior with your specific product’s nuances and brand voice. By creating custom evaluation datasets, Plurai ensures that your LLM application performs exactly how you intend it to, before it ever reaches your users.

## Key Features

*   **Vibe-Train Evals:** Move past basic keyword matching. Plurai allows you to define the "vibe" of your outputs, letting you create custom evaluation criteria that capture the subtlety and tone required for your specific use case.
*   **Context-Aware Guardrails:** Implement robust safety and quality filters that understand your domain. Whether you are building a legal assistant or a creative writing tool, Plurai’s guardrails adapt to your specific constraints.
*   **Seamless Integration:** Designed to fit into modern developer workflows, the platform offers an intuitive interface to manage test cases, iterate on prompts, and monitor performance in real-time.
*   **Dataset Management:** Easily curate and version your evaluation datasets, ensuring consistency as you update your models or fine-tune your prompts.

For indie hackers and solo founders, speed and reliability are everything. When you’re building an AI-powered product, you don’t have a team of 50 to manually QA every response.
