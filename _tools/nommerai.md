## What is Nommer.ai?
Nommer.ai is an AI-powered kitchen assistant designed to transform the cooking experience into a collaborative "2-player mode." It simplifies meal preparation by generating interactive, step-by-step cooking guides that allow two people to coordinate tasks seamlessly in the kitchen.

## Key Features
- **Smart Task Syncing:** Automatically breaks down complex recipes into parallel workflows, assigning specific steps to each person so you aren't bumping into each other.
- **AI Recipe Conversion:** Instantly imports any online recipe and reformats it into a structured, easy-to-follow guide for two cooks.
- **Real-Time Voice Guidance:** Hands-free instructions that keep both players on track without needing to touch your phone with messy fingers.
- **Dynamic Ingredient Scaling:** Adjusts portion sizes and shopping lists instantly, ensuring that your collaborative effort results in the perfect amount of food.

## Verdict
