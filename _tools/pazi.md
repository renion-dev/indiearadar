## What is Pazi?
**Pazi** (https://pazi.ai/) is an AI-powered operations platform designed to bridge the gap between complex business logic and execution. Its tagline, *“Vibe code business operations,”* perfectly captures its philosophy: moving away from rigid, bloated enterprise software toward a more fluid, conversational, and intuitive way of managing workflows. It acts as the "operating system" for your business, allowing you to automate tasks and manage data without needing a degree in software engineering.

## Key Features
*   **Natural Language Workflows:** Instead of building complex logic trees, you describe your business process in plain English, and Pazi translates it into actionable operations.
*   **Unified Data Hub:** Pazi integrates seamlessly with your existing tech stack (CRMs, databases, and communication tools), serving as a single source of truth.
*   **AI-Driven Automation:** It doesn't just trigger events; it makes decisions. Pazi can analyze context, prioritize tasks, and execute complex sequences autonomously.
*   **Low-Code Flexibility:** While "vibe coding" is the core, Pazi provides enough depth for power users to tweak parameters and custom scripts when precision is required.

## Pricing
Pazi currently operates on a tiered model that scales with your business. They offer a **Free Tier** for solo founders and early-stage prototypes, allowing you to test the waters without commitment. Their **Pro and Business tiers** unlock higher API limits, advanced integrations, and priority support, making it a cost-effective alternative to hiring an operations manager or building custom internal tools from scratch.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers and solo founders are the primary champions of Pazi because it solves the "operational bottleneck." When you are a team of one, every minute spent on manual data entry or syncing apps is a minute taken away from product development. Pazi allows these builders to "set it and forget it," effectively giving them a digital assistant that never sleeps. It’s the ultimate leverage for those who want to scale their business without scaling their headcount.

## Verdict
Pazi is a game-changer for lean teams. By turning business operations into a conversation, it democratizes access to high-level automation. If you’re tired of fighting with clunky SaaS integrations and want to focus on shipping, Pazi is an essential addition to your toolkit.
