## What is happycapy?

**Happycapy** is positioning itself as the "agent-native computer for the rest of us." Unlike traditional operating systems that force you to navigate menus and folders, Happycapy treats your computer as a workspace for autonomous agents. It acts as an orchestration layer that allows AI to interact directly with your local files, applications, and browser, turning your PC into an executor rather than just a passive display.

## Key Features

*   **Agent-Native Architecture:** The OS is built from the ground up to support AI agents, allowing them to context-switch between tasks without constant human hand-holding.
*   **Local Automation:** Happycapy executes tasks locally, which is a massive win for privacy-conscious users who don’t want their workflows piped through a generic cloud server.
*   **Unified Interface:** Instead of juggling twenty browser tabs, you interact with a centralized agent interface that handles your repetitive workflows—like data entry, file organization, or report generation—in the background.
*   **Low-Code Integration:** You don’t need to be a Python wizard to get started. Its intuitive design allows users to chain actions together using natural language.

## Pricing

Happycapy currently operates on a **freemium model**. The base version is free for individual users to experiment with local agents. They offer a "Pro" tier for power users and small teams that unlocks unlimited agent workflows, priority processing, and advanced integrations with enterprise APIs.
