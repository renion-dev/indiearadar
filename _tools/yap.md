## What is Yap?
Yap is an open-source, privacy-focused voice dictation tool designed specifically for macOS. By running entirely on-device using Whisper models, it allows you to dictate text into any application without sending your voice data to the cloud.

## Key Features
- **Total Privacy:** Since processing happens locally on your Mac’s silicon, your sensitive conversations never leave your machine.
- **System-Wide Integration:** Works in any text field across macOS, from browsers to code editors and messaging apps.
- **Optimized Performance:** Utilizes Apple’s Neural Engine to ensure fast, responsive transcription without draining your battery.
- **Open-Source Transparency:** The codebase is open for inspection, ensuring no hidden telemetry or data harvesting.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers and developers are flocking to Yap because it solves the "privacy vs. convenience" dilemma. Many dictation tools (like Siri or cloud-based AI) feel intrusive, but Yap provides the power of state-of-the-art speech-to-text without the risk of data leaks. For someone writing documentation, drafting emails, or coding via voice, the ability to keep intellectual property offline while benefiting from high-accuracy AI transcription is a massive productivity unlock. It’s a "set it and forget it" tool that respects the user's local environment.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Privacy-conscious professionals, developers, and writers who want high-quality dictation without cloud-based surveillance.

**Skip if:** You are using an older Mac with an Intel processor (the performance may be sluggish) or if you require advanced multi-language translation features that are currently only available in subscription-heavy, cloud-reliant competitors.
