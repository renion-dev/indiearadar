## What is Fypro?

While TikTok is excellent for viral reach, it is notoriously bad at driving traffic to external storefronts. Fypro solves this by providing a streamlined, mobile-optimized bridge that turns passive scrollers into paying customers without friction.

## Key Features

*   **Smart Bio Links:** Replace generic "link-in-bio" pages with dynamic, conversion-focused storefronts that prioritize your highest-performing products.
*   **Automated DM Funnels:** Trigger automated conversations when users comment on your videos, instantly delivering product links or exclusive discounts to their TikTok inbox.
*   **One-Tap Checkout:** Minimize abandonment rates with a seamless checkout flow that keeps users within the ecosystem, removing the need for clunky browser redirects.
*   **Attribution Tracking:** Finally, see exactly which videos are driving sales. Fypro provides clear analytics linking specific TikTok content to revenue generated.
