## What is qsa.sh ?
qsa.sh is a command-line utility that performs professional-grade external security scans directly from your terminal. It allows developers and sysadmins to instantly audit their public-facing IP addresses for vulnerabilities, open ports, and misconfigurations without needing complex web dashboards.

## Key Features
- **One-Command Execution:** Run a full security audit with a simple `curl` or shell command, no installation or heavy dependencies required.
- **External Attack Surface Mapping:** Identifies exposed services and potential entry points that attackers see from the outside.
- **Real-Time Reporting:** Delivers actionable security insights directly into your terminal output, perfect for CI/CD pipelines or quick sanity checks.
- **Privacy-First Design:** Focused on analyzing your own infrastructure, ensuring that your security data remains local and controlled.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers and solo founders often struggle to justify the cost and overhead of enterprise-grade security suites like Nessus or Qualys. qsa.sh bridges this gap by providing "security-as-a-utility." For a developer launching a new SaaS project, being able to run a quick `qsa.sh` check before deploying to production provides peace of mind that they haven't accidentally left a database port open or a development endpoint exposed to the public internet. It fits perfectly into a terminal-centric workflow, keeping security checks lightweight and frictionless.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Developers, solo founders, and DevOps engineers who prefer CLI tools and need quick, reliable security audits for their infrastructure.

**Skip if:** You require a GUI-based dashboard for compliance reporting, historical trend analysis, or team-wide security policy management.
