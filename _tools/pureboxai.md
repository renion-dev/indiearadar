## What is PureBox.ai?
PureBox.ai is an intelligent email management tool that uses AI to analyze, categorize, and clean up your real Gmail inbox based on your actual reading habits. Instead of generic filters, it learns what you value, allowing you to batch-process newsletters and promotional clutter with a single click.

## Key Features
- **Review-First Cleanup:** Instead of aggressive auto-deletion, PureBox presents a curated digest of your incoming mail, letting you decide what to keep or purge.
- **Smart Categorization:** Automatically sorts emails into "Keep," "Unsubscribe," or "Read Later" buckets using AI-driven context analysis.
- **One-Click Unsubscribe:** Streamlines the process of removing yourself from junk lists that have been clogging your inbox for years.
- **Privacy-Focused:** Designed to work directly with your Gmail API without storing sensitive message content, ensuring your data remains secure.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers and solo founders are notorious for having "inbox zero" anxiety while juggling newsletters, SaaS updates, and customer support tickets. PureBox.ai has become a favorite because it doesn't just block emails—it builds a smarter workflow. By treating the inbox as a "review-first" space, it saves users hours of manual sorting per week, allowing them to focus on shipping code rather than managing marketing noise. It’s the perfect tool for the "time-poor, efficiency-obsessed" crowd.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Busy professionals, freelancers, and startup founders who are overwhelmed by newsletters and want a systematic way to regain control of their Gmail.

**Skip if:** You prefer granular, manual control over every single filter rule or if you use an email client other than Gmail, as this tool is currently optimized specifically for the Google ecosystem.
