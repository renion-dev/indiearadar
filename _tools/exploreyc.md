## What is ExploreYC?

**ExploreYC** is a powerful, developer-first tool that provides structured, real-time access to the company portfolios of top-tier venture capital firms, specifically Y Combinator and a16z. While manually scraping these websites is brittle and time-consuming, ExploreYC acts as a clean, reliable middleman, offering an open-source API that delivers enriched startup data directly into your applications.

## Key Features

*   **Comprehensive Data Sets:** Access structured information on thousands of startups, including company names, descriptions, founders, batch years, and funding stages.
*   **Real-time Updates:** The API is designed to keep pace with new batch announcements, ensuring your lead lists or market research tools are never outdated.
*   **Developer-Friendly:** With well-documented endpoints, developers can integrate the data via REST API in minutes, making it ideal for building internal dashboards or automated outreach systems.
*   **Open-Source Philosophy:** The project encourages community contribution, ensuring transparency and continuous improvement of the data schema.

## Pricing

ExploreYC offers a highly accessible pricing model. Developers can get started with a **free tier** to test the API’s capabilities. For those scaling their projects or requiring higher rate limits, the platform offers affordable premium plans that provide increased request volumes and priority support. It is a cost-effective alternative to expensive, enterprise-grade market intelligence platforms.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It

Indie hackers and solo founders live by the mantra of "building in public" and finding product-market fit quickly. ExploreYC is a goldmine for this community because:
