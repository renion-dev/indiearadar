## What is Lightfield?

Lightfield is an AI-native CRM designed to eliminate the manual grind of data entry and pipeline management. Unlike traditional CRMs that act as digital filing cabinets, Lightfield acts as an autonomous assistant. It observes your workflows, learns your business context, and builds its own database, effectively doing the work for you so you can focus on closing deals rather than updating spreadsheets.

## Key Features

*   **Self-Building Database:** Lightfield automatically extracts and organizes lead information from your communications, meaning no more manual data entry.
*   **Autonomous Workflows:** The AI proactively drafts emails, schedules follow-ups, and updates deal statuses based on real-time interactions.
*   **Contextual Intelligence:** By integrating with your existing tools, it understands the "why" behind your sales process, providing tailored insights rather than generic suggestions.
*   **Zero-UI Philosophy:** The platform is designed to stay out of your way, surfacing only the information you need, exactly when you need it.
