## What is PollyReach?
PollyReach is a powerful communication infrastructure tool designed to bridge the gap between AI agents and the real world. Its core value proposition is simple but transformative: it allows you to assign a dedicated, real phone number to your AI agent, enabling it to initiate and receive voice calls just like a human representative. By providing a seamless API for telephony, PollyReach turns your text-based automation into an active, conversational workforce.

## Key Features
*   **Real Number Integration:** Get a genuine local or toll-free number to give your agent a professional identity.
*   **Voice-to-Voice Pipelines:** High-fidelity audio streaming that ensures low latency, making AI conversations feel natural rather than robotic.
*   **Omnichannel Versatility:** Whether you need to conduct automated sales outreach, appointment setting, or customer support, the platform handles the telephony heavy lifting.
*   **Developer-First API:** Built with integration in mind, it plugs directly into your existing stack, allowing you to trigger calls via webhooks or simple API calls.
*   **Call Analytics:** Track call logs, durations, and outcomes directly through their dashboard to optimize your agent’s performance.

## Pricing
PollyReach offers a flexible, usage-based pricing model that scales with your business. They provide a transparent pay-as-you-go structure, ensuring you only pay for the minutes you use. This makes it an incredibly low-risk entry point for solo founders who want to test voice automation without committing to massive monthly enterprise contracts.
