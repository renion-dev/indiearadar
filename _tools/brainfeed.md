## What is BrainFeed?
BrainFeed is a smart browser extension designed to turn your mindless scrolling habits into a productive learning experience. By intercepting your social media feeds, it replaces distracting content with curated, high-quality educational snippets tailored to your specific interests.

## Key Features
- **Smart Interception:** Automatically detects when you are "doom-scrolling" and injects educational content into your feed.
- **Interest Mapping:** Uses AI to learn what you want to master, whether it’s coding, philosophy, or market trends.
- **Customizable Intensity:** Control how frequently BrainFeed interrupts your scroll to balance productivity with downtime.
- **Curated Knowledge Base:** Sources content from verified, high-signal platforms rather than low-effort viral posts.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers and solo-preneurs are obsessed with BrainFeed because it solves the "context-switching tax" that comes with checking social media for industry news. Instead of getting sucked into the Twitter/X rabbit hole, BrainFeed forces a "micro-learning" moment that keeps your brain in "builder mode" rather than "consumer mode." It transforms a time-sink into a competitive advantage, making it an essential tool for anyone trying to learn while they build.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Developers, lifelong learners, and digital nomads who want to reclaim their attention span without going completely offline.

**Skip if:** You use social media strictly for client acquisition or community management, as the frequent interruptions may interfere with your professional workflow.
