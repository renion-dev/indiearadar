## What is Brila?
Brila is an innovative AI-driven tool designed to bridge the gap between local business reputation and digital presence. Its core value proposition is simple yet powerful: it scrapes your existing Google Maps reviews to generate a fully functional, professional one-page website. By leveraging the authentic feedback already provided by your customers, Brila creates a trust-focused landing page that is ready to publish in minutes.

## Key Features
*   **AI-Powered Content Generation:** Brila analyzes your Google Business Profile to extract key selling points, service details, and customer testimonials, auto-populating your site with relevant copy.
*   **One-Click Publishing:** Eliminate the need for complex drag-and-drop builders. Once the AI generates your site, it’s ready to go live immediately.
*   **SEO Optimization:** Because the content is derived from real customer reviews, the sites are naturally rich in local keywords, helping improve discoverability on search engines.
*   **Mobile-Responsive Design:** Every site created is optimized for mobile users, ensuring potential customers have a seamless experience whether they are on a desktop or a smartphone.
