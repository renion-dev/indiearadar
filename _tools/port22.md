## What is Port22?
Port22 is a mobile-first development environment that brings powerful AI coding assistants, including Claude Code and Codex, directly to your smartphone. It allows developers to manage repositories, execute code, and leverage LLMs for troubleshooting or quick commits while on the go.

## Key Features
- **Mobile AI Integration:** Access top-tier models like Claude and Codex specifically optimized for coding tasks within a mobile interface.
- **SSH & Terminal Access:** Manage your remote servers and codebases directly from your phone with a fully functional terminal emulator.
- **Git Workflow:** Perform commits, pushes, and branch management without needing to open a laptop.
- **Context-Aware Chat:** Use AI to debug code snippets or explain complex logic by pasting directly from your mobile IDE.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers and solo founders are constantly "on call," and Port22 solves the anxiety of being away from a desk during a production outage. Whether you need to push a hotfix, check logs, or ask Claude to optimize a specific function while commuting, Port22 turns your phone into a legitimate workstation. It removes the friction of tethering to a laptop for minor tasks, allowing for a truly asynchronous development lifestyle that fits the "build in public" mentality.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Solo founders, freelance developers, and DevOps engineers who need to manage remote servers or perform emergency code patches while away from their primary workstation.

**Skip if:** You are working on high-security projects where mobile device management (MDM) policies restrict SSH access, or if you prefer the tactile experience of a mechanical keyboard for complex architectural refactoring.
