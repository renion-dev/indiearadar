## What is Goldfish?

Unlike generic AI tools that require constant prompting, Goldfish lives in your workflow, learning your specific voice, context, and project details. By simply pressing **Option** (on Mac), it generates responses that sound authentically like you, saving you from the "blank page" syndrome and the tedious task of drafting routine emails or documentation.
