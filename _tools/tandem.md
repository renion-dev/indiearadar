## What is Tandem?
Tandem is an AI-native brokerage platform designed to streamline the commercial office leasing process for tenants and brokers. By automating property discovery, document analysis, and market comparison, it removes the friction and opacity typically associated with finding office space.

## Key Features
- **AI-Powered Search:** Natural language processing allows you to search for office spaces using descriptive queries rather than rigid filters.
- **Automated Market Analysis:** Instantly generates comparative reports on lease terms and building amenities to help you make data-backed decisions.
- **Intelligent Document Review:** Automatically extracts and flags key clauses in lease agreements to highlight potential risks or hidden costs.
- **Real-Time Inventory Sync:** Provides up-to-date availability and pricing data, reducing the "ghost listing" frustration common in traditional commercial real estate.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers and founders love Tandem because it effectively "disintermediates" the bloated commercial real estate industry. Instead of waiting days for a broker to return an email or provide a curated list of properties, founders can use Tandem to conduct their own due diligence, compare lease structures, and understand market trends in minutes. It transforms a high-cost, high-stress gatekept process into a transparent, self-serve experience, saving both time and hefty commission negotiation headaches.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Founders, startup operators, and independent brokers looking to cut out the middlemen and accelerate the office search process.

**Skip if:** You are looking for a high-touch, "white-glove" service where you prefer to outsource the entire negotiation and site-visit process to a traditional human-only brokerage firm.
