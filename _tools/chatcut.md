## What is ChatCut?

**ChatCut** is an all-in-one AI video editing ecosystem designed to bridge the gap between prompt engineering and professional video production. By integrating directly into ChatGPT via a custom GPT, while also offering dedicated desktop and web applications, it allows users to transform text, URLs, or raw footage into polished, social-ready videos in seconds. Whether you are a content creator, marketer, or developer, ChatCut acts as your AI-powered production assistant.

## Key Features

*   **Multi-Platform Flexibility:** Seamlessly switch between the ChatGPT plugin for quick ideation, the web app for browser-based editing, and the desktop app for heavy-duty tasks.
*   **Text-to-Video Engine:** Simply input a topic or a script, and ChatCut generates a storyboard, selects relevant stock assets, and applies professional transitions automatically.
*   **AI Voiceovers & Subtitles:** Generate high-quality, human-like voiceovers and auto-sync captions in multiple languages with a single click.
*   **Smart Trimming:** Automatically remove silences, filler words, and long pauses from your raw footage to tighten up your pacing.
*   **Brand Kits:** Maintain consistency across your content by saving your unique fonts, colors, and logos.

## Pricing

ChatCut offers a **freemium model** that allows users to test the AI capabilities without an upfront investment. 
*   **Free Tier:** Perfect for casual creators, allowing for basic video generation with watermarks.
*   **Pro Plan:** A monthly subscription that unlocks high-definition exports, removal of watermarks, advanced stock media libraries, and priority processing.
*   **Enterprise:** Custom solutions for teams requiring collaboration tools and API access.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It

Indie hackers are flocking to ChatCut because it solves the "content bottleneck." Many solo founders struggle to produce high-quality marketing videos due to the steep learning curve of traditional software like Premiere Pro. ChatCut’s low-friction, prompt-based workflow allows developers to ship promotional videos for their products during their lunch break. It turns video production into a simple task of "editing text" rather than "editing timelines."
