## What is Honestly?

**Honestly** is an AI-powered market research tool designed to cut through the noise of social media. Instead of relying on vanity metrics or curated marketing feedback, Honestly scans Reddit and TikTok to provide unfiltered, raw sentiment about your product, brand, or niche. It turns the chaotic, fast-paced nature of social discourse into actionable insights, helping founders understand exactly how the public perceives their work.

## Key Features

*   **Social Sentiment Analysis:** Instantly aggregates discussions from Reddit and TikTok to identify trends, pain points, and praise.
*   **Competitor Benchmarking:** See how your competitors are being talked about and find gaps in their offerings that you can exploit.
*   **AI Summarization:** Instead of manually scrolling through thousands of comments, get concise, human-readable reports on what users truly care about.
*   **Real-time Alerts:** Get notified when a new thread or video starts gaining traction regarding your specific keywords.
*   **Actionable Feedback Loops:** Translate social sentiment directly into product roadmap priorities.

## Pricing

Honestly offers a tiered subscription model designed to scale with your business:
*   **Free Trial:** Explore the dashboard and see limited insights.
*   **Pro Plan:** Best for indie hackers and solo founders, providing deep-dive reports and daily monitoring.
*   **Enterprise:** Custom solutions for agencies and larger teams requiring API access and unlimited keyword tracking.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It

For indie hackers, "market validation" is often a guessing game. Honestly removes the guesswork. By surfacing what people are *actually* saying on platforms where users are notoriously blunt (like Reddit), founders can validate features before building them. It saves countless hours of manual research and provides the kind of brutal honesty that prevents wasted development time. It’s essentially a "listening device" for your target audience.

If you’re tired of the echo chamber of traditional marketing, **Honestly** is a game-changer. It bridges the gap between cold data and human emotion.
