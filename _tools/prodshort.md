## What is ProdShort?

**ProdShort** is an AI-powered content repurposing engine designed specifically for creators, founders, and teams who want to maximize the ROI of their meetings. Instead of letting valuable insights disappear into a cloud recording, ProdShort transforms your Zoom, Google Meet, or uploaded video files into polished, ready-to-post short-form videos and written social media content. It bridges the gap between "getting work done" and "building in public."

## Key Features

*   **Smart Clipping:** Automatically identifies the most engaging, high-value segments from long-form recordings.
*   **AI-Powered Editing:** Trims silences, adds dynamic captions, and formats videos to vertical 9:16 aspect ratios automatically.
*   **Multi-Platform Output:** Generates not just clips, but also optimized LinkedIn posts, Twitter threads, and blog summaries based on the meeting transcript.
*   **Brand Personalization:** Customize the look and feel of your captions and layouts to match your personal brand aesthetic.
*   **Seamless Integration:** Sync directly with your meeting platforms to automate your content workflow.
