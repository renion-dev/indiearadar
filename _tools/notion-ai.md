## What is Notion AI?

Notion AI brings the power of large language models directly into your workspace. Instead of switching between ChatGPT and your notes, you get AI assistance right where you work.

## Key Features

- **AI Writer** — Generate blog posts, emails, and meeting notes from bullet points
- **Auto-fill** — Extract action items and summaries from messy notes
- **Q&A** — Ask questions about your entire knowledge base and get instant answers
- **Translate** — Convert content to 10+ languages without leaving the page
