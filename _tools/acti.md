## What is Acti?
Acti is an "agentic keyboard" for mobile that transforms how you interact with your smartphone. Unlike standard keyboards that simply input text, Acti acts as a command layer for your device. By integrating directly into your mobile experience, it allows you to execute complex tasks, perform deep searches, and automate workflows without ever switching apps. It essentially turns your keyboard into a personal assistant.

## Key Features
*   **Agentic Command Interface:** Type natural language commands to perform actions across different apps, such as "Find my flight to Tokyo" or "Draft a summary of this email."
*   **Unified Search:** Skip the browser. Acti pulls information from your apps and the web simultaneously, presenting answers directly in the keyboard interface.
*   **Context Awareness:** Acti understands what you are doing on-screen, providing relevant suggestions and tools based on your current task.
*   **Privacy-First Architecture:** Designed with security in mind, ensuring that your data stays local or encrypted while the agent processes your requests.
*   **Seamless Integration:** It replaces your default iOS/Android keyboard, meaning it works anywhere you can type.
