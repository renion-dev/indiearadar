## What is Leaping AI?
Leaping AI is an automation platform that deploys intelligent AI agents capable of executing multi-day outreach campaigns via phone calls and text messages. It bridges the gap between static chatbots and human sales reps by handling complex, persistent follow-up sequences that nurture leads over time.

## Key Features
*   **Multi-Channel Sequences:** Automatically orchestrates a mix of voice calls and SMS follow-ups over several days.
*   **Natural Conversational AI:** Agents are trained to handle objections, answer FAQs, and maintain context throughout long-running conversations.
*   **Real-Time Lead Routing:** Seamlessly passes "hot" leads to your human team the moment an agent identifies genuine interest.
*   **Customizable Scripts:** Allows users to define specific brand voices and goal-oriented workflows for different outreach scenarios.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers are flocking to Leaping AI because it solves the "lead ghosting" problem without the overhead of hiring a full-time SDR. For instance, a founder can set up a campaign to reach out to cold leads, have the AI handle the initial qualification via text, and only jump into the conversation once a meeting is booked. It effectively turns a manual, soul-crushing follow-up process into a "set it and forget it" engine, allowing small teams to punch far above their weight class in terms of lead conversion.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Solopreneurs, small agency owners, and lean sales teams who need to nurture leads consistently without manual labor.

**Skip if:** You are in a highly regulated industry where every word of a voice conversation requires strict legal compliance/recording, or if you prefer a high-touch, human-only sales approach for every single contact.
