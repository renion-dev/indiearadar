## What is Conduit?
Conduit is a specialized AI agent platform designed specifically for the hospitality industry. It automates complex guest interactions, reservation management, and operational workflows by integrating directly with existing hospitality tech stacks.

## Key Features
- **Context-Aware Booking:** Handles reservations and inquiries with deep knowledge of room types, amenities, and local policies.
- **PMS Integration:** Syncs seamlessly with property management systems to pull real-time availability and guest profile data.
- **Multilingual Support:** Provides instant, natural-sounding communication for international guests across various messaging channels.
- **Operational Autonomy:** Not just a chatbot; it can trigger tasks like scheduling housekeeping or alerting maintenance based on guest requests.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers and boutique hoteliers love Conduit because it solves the "always-on" problem without the overhead of a 24/7 front desk team. Unlike generic LLM wrappers, Conduit is purpose-built to navigate the specific nuances of hospitality—such as handling late check-ins, local recommendations, and service recovery—without hallucinating policies. For a solo operator managing multiple listings, it effectively functions as a digital concierge, drastically reducing response times and increasing direct booking conversions by handling the manual "grunt work" of guest messaging.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Boutique hotel owners, Airbnb property managers, and hospitality startups looking to automate guest experience at scale.

**Skip if:** You run a very small, low-volume rental (under 3 units) where personal, manual interaction is your primary competitive advantage, or if your tech stack is entirely analog.
