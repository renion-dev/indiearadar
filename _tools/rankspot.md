## What is RankSpot?

**RankSpot** is an AI-powered content automation platform designed to bridge the gap between technical SEO and high-performing blog content. Unlike generic AI writers that simply "spin" text, RankSpot operates on deep competitor intelligence. By analyzing top-ranking pages for your target keywords, it reverse-engineers the search intent and structural requirements needed to climb the SERPs, allowing you to generate fully optimized, ready-to-publish blog posts in minutes.

## Key Features

*   **Deep Competitor Analysis:** RankSpot scans the top 10 results for your keyword, extracting the headers, keyword density, and topical gaps your competitors are missing.
*   **SEO-First Content Generation:** The AI doesn't just write; it integrates semantic keywords, internal linking structures, and meta descriptions automatically.
*   **Brand Voice Customization:** You can feed the AI your brand’s tone and style guide to ensure the output sounds human and consistent.
*   **One-Click Publishing:** Seamless integration with CMS platforms like WordPress allows you to push content from the editor to your site instantly.

## Pricing
