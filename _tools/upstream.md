## What is Upstream?
Upstream is a next-generation email client that fundamentally reimagines how we interact with our inboxes. Positioned as "the inbox designed for humans and agents," it acts as a bridge between traditional communication and the burgeoning world of AI automation. Instead of just organizing your mail, Upstream treats your inbox as a workspace where AI agents can operate alongside you to triage, draft, and execute tasks.

## Key Features
*   **Agent-First Architecture:** Upstream allows you to deploy specialized AI agents to handle repetitive tasks like scheduling, customer support responses, or newsletter filtering.
*   **Intelligent Triage:** The platform automatically prioritizes emails based on context rather than just sender reputation, ensuring you see what matters most first.
*   **Human-in-the-Loop Workflow:** The UI is built for speed, allowing you to review and approve AI-generated drafts with a single click, keeping you in control without the manual labor.
*   **Unified Context:** It integrates seamlessly with your existing tech stack, pulling data from other apps to give your AI agents the context needed to provide accurate, personalized replies.

## Pricing
Upstream currently operates on a tiered subscription model:
*   **Free Tier:** Ideal for individuals testing the waters with basic AI triage.
*   **Pro Tier:** Geared toward power users and freelancers, offering higher usage limits for AI agents and advanced integrations.
*   **Teams:** Designed for collaborative environments, featuring shared AI workflows and administrative controls.
