## What is Liminal?
Liminal is an AI-native workspace designed to function as a collaborative "second brain" for individuals and teams. It bridges the gap between static documentation and active execution by integrating an intelligent agent directly into your workflow to manage, synthesize, and act on your data.

## Key Features
*   **Agentic Workflows:** Deploy autonomous agents that can execute tasks, summarize long threads, and retrieve information across your connected apps.
*   **Unified Knowledge Base:** Centralizes scattered notes, documents, and communications into a searchable, context-aware environment.
*   **Context-Aware Collaboration:** Allows team members to share insights with the AI, ensuring the "second brain" evolves with the team’s collective knowledge.
*   **Cross-Platform Integration:** Syncs seamlessly with popular productivity stacks to pull in data without requiring manual migration.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers are gravitating toward Liminal because it solves the "context switching" tax that plagues solo founders. Instead of spending hours digging through Slack threads, Notion pages, and emails to find a specific decision or project requirement, users can simply query their Liminal agent. It acts as a force multiplier for a one-person team, effectively automating the "project manager" role so the founder can focus on shipping code or content rather than managing fragmented information.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Solopreneurs, small startup teams, and knowledge workers who feel overwhelmed by information fragmentation and want an AI partner that actually "remembers" their work.

**Skip if:** You prefer a low-tech, file-based organizational system (like plain Markdown files) or if your data security requirements prohibit using third-party AI integrations for proprietary information.
