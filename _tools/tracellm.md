## What is TraceLLM?
TraceLLM is an observability platform designed specifically for the AI stack, providing OpenTelemetry-based tracing to monitor production LLM applications. It allows developers to visualize complex prompt chains, track latency, and debug token usage in real-time.

## Key Features
- **End-to-End Tracing:** Visualize the entire lifecycle of a request, from initial prompt to final model output, pinpointing exactly where bottlenecks occur.
- **LLM-Specific Metrics:** Track cost, token consumption, and latency per request to optimize your budget and user experience.
- **Full-Stack Integration:** Seamlessly integrates with existing OpenTelemetry standards, making it easy to plug into your current infrastructure.
- **Automated Alerting:** Set custom thresholds for error rates or abnormal latency to get notified before your users notice a problem.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers building AI wrappers or complex agents often struggle with the "black box" nature of LLMs—it’s notoriously difficult to know why a model hallucinated or why a chain failed in production. TraceLLM solves this by providing a clear, granular view of the execution flow. For a solo dev, this means less time guessing why a prompt failed and more time shipping features, as they can quickly isolate whether a failure was due to a model timeout, a bad prompt, or an API error.

## Verdict
**Best for:** AI engineers and indie developers who need visibility into their LLM chains to optimize performance and troubleshoot production errors.

**Skip if:** You are building simple, static prompt applications that don't require complex orchestration or if you are strictly focused on local development without a production deployment.
