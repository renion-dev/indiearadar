## What is RecipeBook by Shofo?
RecipeBook by Shofo is a specialized platform that provides high-quality, structured video training data for AI developers and researchers. With a library of over 25 million clips, it allows users to purchase specific hours of labeled video content to accelerate the training of computer vision and multimodal AI models.

- **Massive Scale:** Access to 25M+ categorized video clips to ensure model diversity and robustness.
- **Hourly Licensing:** A flexible "buy by the hour" model that lets you scale your data acquisition based on your current project budget.
- **Structured Metadata:** Clips are organized with precise annotations, making them "plug-and-play" for machine learning pipelines.
- **High-Diversity Datasets:** Access to a wide variety of real-world scenarios, essential for reducing bias in AI training.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers and solo AI developers often hit a wall when they need high-quality training data because manual scraping is time-consuming and legally complex. RecipeBook by Shofo solves this by acting as a "data supermarket." Instead of spending months collecting and labeling raw footage, developers can purchase precisely what they need, allowing them to iterate on their model architecture in days rather than months. It’s a massive time-saver for those building niche vision applications who don’t have an in-house data collection team.

## Verdict
