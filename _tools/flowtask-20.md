## What is FlowTask 2.0?
FlowTask 2.0 acts as a centralized "company brain," allowing AI agents to access, process, and act upon your team’s internal documentation and workflows. It bridges the gap between static knowledge bases and autonomous execution, ensuring your AI agents have the context they need to perform complex tasks accurately.

## Key Features
* **Unified Knowledge Graph:** Automatically indexes your Slack, Notion, and Google Drive files into a searchable, agent-ready vector database.
* **Contextual Agent Routing:** Dynamically assigns tasks to the best-suited AI agent based on the specific knowledge domain required.
* **Human-in-the-Loop Approval:** Provides an intuitive dashboard to review, edit, or reject agent-generated outputs before they go live.
* **Real-time Sync:** Ensures that whenever a policy or document is updated, your AI agents immediately reflect those changes in their reasoning.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers are flocking to FlowTask 2.0 because it solves the "context window" nightmare. Instead of manually feeding prompt instructions every time you start a new task, you simply point FlowTask at your startup's documentation. One solo founder reported using it to automate customer support responses that sound exactly like their brand voice, simply by indexing their past support tickets and product roadmap. It essentially allows a single developer to run a "team" of specialized AI agents without the management overhead.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Solopreneurs, early-stage startup founders, and operations teams looking to automate repetitive, knowledge-heavy workflows without constant manual oversight.

**Skip if:** You don’t have a centralized documentation system (like Notion or GitHub) in place yet; FlowTask is only as smart as the data you feed it. If your company knowledge is scattered across sticky notes and unorganized emails, you'll need to clean house before this tool becomes useful.
