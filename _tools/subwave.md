## What is SUB/WAVE?
SUB/WAVE is a self-hosted radio platform that transforms your digital music collection into a live, linear listening experience. By integrating an AI DJ, it creates a seamless, curated broadcast that you can share with friends or listeners via a single, persistent stream.

## Key Features
- **Self-Hosted Control:** Host your own music library (FLAC, MP3, etc.) on your server, ensuring full ownership of your media without cloud-provider restrictions.
- **AI-Powered DJ:** Features an intelligent AI personality that introduces tracks, provides commentary, and bridges the gap between songs just like a traditional radio host.
- **Unified Stream:** Generate a single, live URL that anyone can tune into, perfect for shared listening parties or community radio stations.
- **Seamless Transitions:** Uses intelligent beat-matching and crossfading to ensure your music library feels like a professional broadcast rather than a static playlist.
