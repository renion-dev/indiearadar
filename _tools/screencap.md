## What is Screencap?
Screencap is a productivity tool that captures your team’s actual software workflows and converts them into structured AI training data. By recording your screen while you work, it automatically generates the documentation, prompts, and datasets needed to train custom AI agents on your company’s specific processes.

## Key Features
- **Workflow Capture:** Records your screen and mouse movements to document how you interact with specific apps and web tools.
- **Auto-Documentation:** Instantly transforms raw screen recordings into step-by-step guides and text-based operational procedures.
- **Dataset Generation:** Packages your team’s "tribal knowledge" into clean, structured datasets ready to train LLMs or custom internal bots.
- **Privacy Controls:** Allows you to redact sensitive information automatically during the recording process to ensure security.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers and solo founders often struggle with the "documentation debt" that accumulates when trying to scale or hand off tasks to virtual assistants. Screencap solves this by removing the friction of manual writing; you simply record yourself performing a task once, and the tool handles the heavy lifting of turning that session into a repeatable AI instruction set. It’s an essential bridge for founders looking to automate their operations without spending hours writing SOPs from scratch.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Founders, operations managers, and small teams looking to delegate tasks to AI or new hires by turning "how-to" sessions into structured training manuals.

**Skip if:** You don’t have repetitive, process-heavy workflows or if your team rarely uses browser-based software tools, as the value of the tool diminishes in non-digital environments.
