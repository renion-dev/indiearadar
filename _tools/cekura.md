## What is Cekura?
Cekura is an AI-powered quality assurance and optimization platform designed specifically for voice agents. It acts as a "self-improvement loop" by automatically evaluating call transcripts, identifying performance gaps, and providing actionable insights to refine agent behavior over time.

## Key Features
- **Automated QA Scoring:** Instantly grade voice agent interactions based on custom rubrics, compliance requirements, and tone.
- **Continuous Feedback Loop:** Automatically feeds evaluation data back into your agent’s prompt engineering workflow to improve future performance.
- **Sentiment & Intent Analysis:** Deep-dives into caller emotions and intent to detect where agents lose rapport or fail to resolve queries.
- **Drift Detection:** Identifies when an agent begins to deviate from its intended persona or knowledge base.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers and solo founders building voice-first products often struggle with the "black box" nature of LLMs. Cekura solves the manual nightmare of listening to hundreds of call recordings to figure out why an agent isn't closing sales or resolving tickets. By automating the auditing process, it allows a single developer to maintain the quality of a full customer support team, ensuring that their voice agent actually improves with every interaction rather than stagnating.

## Verdict
**Best for:** AI developers, customer experience managers, and indie founders who have deployed voice agents and are struggling to optimize conversion or resolution rates.

**Skip if:** You are still in the early prototyping phase, have very low call volume, or are using a basic rule-based IVR system that doesn't utilize LLMs.
