## What is tablo?
Tablo is an observability and monitoring tool designed specifically for AI coding agents. Think of it as a "digital watchdog" that sits in your workspace, tracking agent behavior, cost, and output quality to ensure your automated coding assistants don’t go off the rails.

## Key Features
- **Real-time Agent Oversight:** Live tracking of what your AI agents are doing, allowing you to catch hallucinations or infinite loops before they become expensive.
- **Cost & Token Analytics:** Deep insights into token consumption, helping you optimize prompts and avoid "bill shock" from heavy LLM usage.
- **Contextual Debugging:** Detailed logs that map agent actions to specific code changes, making it easy to revert or fix errors introduced by an agent.
- **Framework Agnostic:** Works seamlessly with popular agentic frameworks, ensuring you have a unified dashboard regardless of your tech stack.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
Indie hackers are increasingly relying on autonomous agents like Devin, Cursor, or AutoGPT to ship MVPs at breakneck speeds. However, the "black box" nature of these tools often leads to silent failures or unexpected API costs. Tablo bridges this trust gap, providing the transparency needed to let an agent run in the background without constantly hovering over the terminal. It gives solo founders the peace of mind to focus on high-level architecture while Tablo keeps the "tiny cat" watch on the implementation details.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Solo developers, agency owners, and engineering teams currently integrating autonomous coding agents into their daily development workflow.

**Skip if:** You are still writing 100% of your code manually or if your current project is too small to justify the overhead of managing an observability layer.
