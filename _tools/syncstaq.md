## What is SyncStaq?
SyncStaq is a streamlined automation tool that bridges the gap between Stripe and Google Sheets by automatically syncing your billing data in real-time. It eliminates the need for manual CSV exports or complex API integrations, ensuring your financial reporting and customer tracking are always up-to-date.

## Key Features
- **Real-time Sync:** Data flows from Stripe to your spreadsheet automatically without manual intervention.
- **Customizable Schemas:** Choose exactly which data points (subscriptions, invoices, customers) you want to track.
- **Historical Data Backfill:** Quickly import your existing Stripe history into new or existing sheets.
- **No-Code Setup:** Connect your Stripe account via API key and start syncing in under two minutes.

## Why Indie Hackers Love It
For indie hackers, time is the most valuable currency. Before SyncStaq, managing MRR tracking or churn analysis meant spending hours every Monday morning manually exporting CSVs from the Stripe dashboard. With SyncStaq, founders can build live-updating dashboards in Google Sheets or Looker Studio, allowing them to track growth metrics, identify failed payments, and monitor customer lifetime value without writing a single line of code. It turns a manual administrative burden into a "set it and forget it" workflow, freeing up time to actually build the product.

## Verdict
**Best for:** Solopreneurs, SaaS founders, and small teams who rely on Google Sheets for financial reporting and want to automate their data pipeline.

**Skip if:** You already use a robust, high-end BI tool (like ChartMogul or Baremetrics) that provides these dashboards natively, or if your business model doesn't rely on subscription-based billing.
